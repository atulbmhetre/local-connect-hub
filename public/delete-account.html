Delete Your Account

App: Aaspaas Pro  ·  Last updated: 6 September 2026

You can request deletion of your Aaspaas Pro account and associated personal data using either method below. You do not need to install the app to use the email option.

1. Delete in the app (recommended)

If you have the app installed and are signed in with your phone number:

  1. Open Aaspaas Pro.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps.

Customer accounts are deleted promptly. Vendor accounts enter a scheduled deletion period (typically 30 days) so you can finish existing orders or cancel the request from Settings.

2. Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address you use for support (or any email you can receive replies on) and include your registered 10-digit mobile number so we can verify ownership:

Email: support@aaspaaspro.com

Subject: Account deletion request

Include: Your registered phone number (+91 …)

We will process verified requests within a reasonable timeframe. See our Privacy Policy for retention details.
